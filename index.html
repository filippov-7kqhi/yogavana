About Yogavana

A Practice Rooted in Tradition

Founded by 500hr-trained teacher Devika Rao, Yogavana was built as a sanctuary for traditional practice — Hatha yoga, pranayama and Ayurvedic wisdom taught with the depth and patience these traditions deserve, not rushed or diluted for a trend.

  • 500hr traditionally trained yoga instructors
  • Hatha, Yin, meditation and pranayama practices
  • Ayurveda-inspired wellness guidance
  • Multi-day retreats in a quiet, natural setting
